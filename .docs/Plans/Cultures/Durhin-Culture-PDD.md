**Project:** Ninth Age

**Status:** Initial established lore / substantial open design  
**Scope:** Durhîn (Dark Children), Kabithh's patronage, partial Withering, Hara-Ur and the northern Durhîn lands

# 1. Purpose and Authority

The **Durhîn (Dark Children)** are a distinct Aurësén-descended people. This document records their established culture and identifies what has **not** yet been designed. It is subordinate to the [Culture Design PDD](../Culture-Design-PDD.md), with shared historical truth in [World Lore and History](../World-Lore-and-History-PDD.md). Do not infer playable race, classes, faction mechanics, architecture or typical NPC designs from this brief.

# 2. Origins and Condition — Established

The Durhîn are descended from a **group of Aurësén who swore service to Kabithh**, Lady of Deceit of the Winter Court, **in return for partially embracing the Withering**.

Unlike Arhîn afflicted by the Withering, the Durhîn **retain their physical bodies**. Their sanity is **only partially preserved**. They are continually subject to **negative, typically violent emotional states**. They are not simply ordinary healthy Arhîn liberated from the need for sunlight, nor fully disembodied Withered shadows. **The Durhîn retain the same underlying physical proportions and facial anatomy as the Arhîn and Cehîn**. Their partial Withering does not establish a different body build or facial structure; other aspects of their appearance remain to be defined. **Reactivation of the Annokal (Gift of Light) makes a remedy for their partially Withered condition available**, as for all other peoples descended from the Aurësén. **Individual Durhîn must accept the restored Gift** to receive its protection and healing; merely reactivating the Annokal does not automatically cure them. Their pact with Kabithh does not exclude them from accepting this ancestral protection. Whether acceptance reverses other physical distinctions, and how it affects political allegiance or social organisation, remain undecided. The exact magical mechanisms of Kabithh's patronage, their physical ageing, reproduction and any light requirements are not established.

Kabithh is their **patron** and is **revered** by the Durhîn. By contrast, her worship among the Arhîn is **heavily forbidden**, and the Arhîn regard her as an enemy.

# 3. Geography and Civilisation — Established

The Durhîn possess **their own civilisation** in the **northern tundra and taiga**, **north of Arataure**. Their capital is the **citadel of Hara-Ur**.

The Durhîn and Arhîn hold intense **mutual hatred**. No present treaty, trade arrangement, leadership title, other settlements or exact frontiers have yet been established.

# 4. Design Boundaries and Open Decisions

**Established visual minimum:** physically embodied descendants of Aurësén, dwelling in northern tundra/taiga, emotionally afflicted yet not wholly mindless; capital at Hara-Ur. Detailed skin, eye, hair, clothing, heraldry, building materials and military identity must not be invented as canon.

**Proposed possibilities only:** practical northern-climate clothing and defensible northern architecture consistent with the climate, while preserving continuity with Aurësén ancestry. Any distinctively Kabithh-associated symbols, ritual garments, magical effects or Withering-related changes require explicit decisions.

**Further consideration required:**
- Political ruler(s), hierarchy, laws, governance, independence from or control by Kabithh.
- Exact nature of the partial Withering, its permanence and relationship with sunlight and magic.
- Daily life, family, economy, religion, cultural arts, training and military institutions.
- Hara-Ur architecture and other settlements; borders, interaction with other northern peoples.
- Relationship of Durhîn to Ythralla and other Grey Ones, and whether dissenting Durhîn exist.
- Character appearance, population and lifecycle, cultural differentiation and asset families.
