## Interpretation and reuse

The images establish **silhouette, material language and medium-scale detailing**: curved smooth pale plaster, gold architectural trims, controlled stone foundations, hand-painted grounded mid-poly appearance, with blue-grey glass roofs on the upper storey. The detached and urban concept images are **visual references**, not finished geometric build specifications.

The ground-floor system is intended to be assembled from atomic parts and to allow each arcade bay to sit at an **independent street elevation** for sloped roads. Do not infer fixed dimensions, a universal arch geometry, or a mandatory level upper floor solely from these images. Follow the Graphical Approach PDD and future approved modular dimensions.

Excluded from this folder: superseded/rejected multi-panel concept-art sheets and unapproved early experiments. Additional orthographic views are separately requested deliverables, not part of the default single three-quarter-view concept.
